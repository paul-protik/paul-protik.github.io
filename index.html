Protik Kumar Paul

Postdoctoral researcher in the Zero-Knowledge Group at the University of Tartu. I build secure multiparty computation and zero-knowledge protocols that hold up in practice.

Research

My research covers secure multiparty computation (MPC), zero-knowledge (ZK) proofs, private function evaluation (PFE) and privacy-preserving frameworks.

I design efficient protocols under demanding adversarial models and turn them into working systems. I developed the Asterisk and QuickPool frameworks, and I work in C++ with EMP-toolkit and ABY.

At TU Darmstadt, I co-supervised a PhD student on authenticated private function evaluation. I also supervised an M.Tech student on fair, helper-aided MPC with cheater identification, together with Dr. Divya Ravi (University of Amsterdam).
